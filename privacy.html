Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026

CivilQuest is a learning app for civil engineering, made by Convironment, LLC, a Nebraska, USA limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information CivilQuest collects, why, and what control you have over it. It applies to the CivilQuest student app (on the web, Android and iOS) and the CivilQuest instructor portal.

The short version

  • Students don’t create accounts. We store the name a student types, the class they join, and their level results, so their instructor can see them.
  • We don’t show ads, sell personal information, or track people across other apps or websites.
  • Instructors can see results for their own classes only.

What we collect

FromWhatWhy
Students who join a classThe name they type, the class code, a random anonymous identifier created by the app, and each finished level’s result (course, chapter, level, stars, number of mistakes, the types of mistakes made, and the time)To show the instructor how the class is doing and which ideas need more teaching
Students practising without a classNothing is sent to us. Progress is stored only on the device.To remember stars and progress on that device
InstructorsName, institution, email address, password (stored securely by our sign-in provider; we never see it), classes created, invite code used, and plan statusTo run the instructor account, classes and trials or subscriptions
EveryoneBasic technical data that web and app services process automatically, such as IP address and device or browser typeTo deliver the service securely and prevent abuse

The app also keeps some information on your own device (for example your name, class code, stars, and results waiting to be sent while offline). You can remove it by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it.

Who can see student results

Only the instructor who owns the class a student joined. Other students cannot see anyone’s results, and instructors cannot see classes they don’t own. Our security rules enforce this on the server.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores accounts and results. Data is stored in Google Cloud’s Middle East region (Doha, Qatar) and may be processed elsewhere by Google under its own safeguards.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website version of the app and portal.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces on the website version.
  • When paid instructor plans open, Stripe will process payments. We will not receive or store card numbers.

These providers process data on our behalf and only to provide their services.

How long we keep data

Student results are kept until the instructor deletes the class (which deletes its results) or until we receive a deletion request. Instructor accounts are kept while they are in use; to delete an instructor account and all its classes and results, email us from the account’s address and we will do so within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete information about you. Students can also ask their instructor, who can delete results from their class. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR or US state privacy laws; we will honour them. Contact us at the address below.

Children

CivilQuest is designed for university and college students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used CivilQuest, contact us and we will delete their information.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access is limited by server-side security rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app or portal.
